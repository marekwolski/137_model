# Reference model for the modern digital workplace

This is my 1-3-7 reference model for the modern digital workplace.

It addresses the scope and purpose of the modern digital workplace, the three Outcome Domains or "Experiences" through which we can decide what good needs to look like and describes the seven core links in the Value Chain that exist in any worthwhile modern digital workplace.

Start here for the [Introduction]({{site.baseurl}}/introduction).

I decided to write this guide after failing to find anything like it:

- that wasn't compromised by the need of a vendor to sell their stuff

- or skewed by someone dazzled by the latest wizzy thing.

I work as an Enterprise Architect for a systems integrator and it's my job to collect **all** the pieces of a solution that are required and knit them together into something **that works for all the stakeholders**.

If you have any comments or questions then I'd love to hear from you, so please do get in contact. If you have something you'd like to contribute then, again, please do contact me and let's see if we can put the power of GitHub to good use!
