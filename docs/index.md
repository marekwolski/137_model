# The Official Guide to the 1-3-7 Model

## A reference model for the modern digital workplace



This is the official guide to my 1-3-7 reference model for the modern digital workplace.

It addresses:
- "1": the [Purpose]({{site.baseurl}}/purpose) of the modern digital workplace
- "3": the three [Outcome Domains]({{site.baseurl}}/outcome_domains) or "experiences" through which we can decide what good needs to look like
- "7": the seven [Capability Areas]({{site.baseurl}}/capability_areas) that comprise the modern digital workplace.

Start here for the [Introduction]({{site.baseurl}}/introduction).



## Why ... ?

I decided to write this guide after failing to find anything like it that wasn't compromised by the need of a vendor to sell their stuff or skewed by someone dazzled by the latest wizzy thing.

I work as a Chief Architect for a systems integrator on a large digital workplace services contract. It's my job to collect **all** the pieces for solutions that are required and knit them together into something **that works for all the stakeholders**.

I wanted (needed!) some sort of reference model so it was easier to:
- describe the current state of the digital workplace that I help to maintain and develop
- describe desired target states of the digital workplace system and what makes them desireable
- create accurate gap analyses to define transformation opportunities
- keep a view of the "big picture".

So far, my 1-3-7 Model seems to be meeting those needs.
