# Reference model for the modern digital workplace

This is my 1-3-7 reference model for the modern digital workplace.

It addresses:
- "1": the purpose of the modern digital workplace,
- "3": the three Outcome Domains or "experiences" through which we can decide what good needs to look like
- "7": the seven core links in the Value Chain that comprise the modern digital workplace.

Start here for the [Introduction]({{site.baseurl}}/introduction).

## Why ... ?

I decided to write this guide after failing to find anything like it:
- that wasn't compromised by the need of a vendor to sell their stuff
- or skewed by someone dazzled by the latest wizzy thing.

I work as a Chief Architect for a systems integrator on a large digital workplace services contract. It's my job to collect **all** the pieces for solutions that are required and knit them together into something **that works for all the stakeholders**.

I wanted some sort of reference model so it was easier to:
- describe the current state of the digital workplace I help to maintain and develop
- describe desired target states of the digital workplace system and what makes them desireable
- create accurate gap analyses to define transformation opportunities
- keep a view of the "big picture".
